Privacy Policy

GetMods Browser · Last updated: November 25, 2026

GetMods Browser ("the app") is made by Anas Studio ("we", "us"). This page explains what information the app uses and why. We keep it short and plain.

Information we collect

  • Account details. If you sign in with Google, GitHub or email, we receive your name, email address and profile photo from that sign-in. Email sign-in stores your email address and a password (kept encrypted by our login provider, Supabase). We use these only to sign you in and show your profile.
  • Crash reports. If the app closes unexpectedly, it can send a report with your phone model, Android version, app version, the screen you were on, and technical error details. It does not include your browsing history.
  • Feedback. If you send feedback, we store your message and the contact you choose to add.
  • Searches. Web searches go through our own search service (GetMods Search). We do not build profiles of users or sell search data.

Data kept on your phone

Your browsing history, bookmarks, saved passwords, downloads and settings are stored on your device. We do not receive them.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Start.io. The ad provider may collect device and advertising identifiers to show and measure ads, under its own privacy policy.

How we use information

  • To sign you in and show your profile.
  • To find and fix crashes and bugs.
  • To read and answer your feedback.
  • To show notices and updates inside the app.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. It is shared only with the services that run the app: Supabase (sign-in and storage), Google or GitHub (if you choose them to sign in), and Start.io (ads).

Your choices and deleting your data

You can sign out at any time from the Account page. To have your account and stored data deleted, message us on Telegram at t.me/GetMods_offecial from the account you signed up with, and we will remove it.

Children

The app is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above.

Contact

Questions? Reach us on Telegram: t.me/GetMods_offecial.
